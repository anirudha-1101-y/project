Questions



Find the total number of students in each class.

mysql> select class,count(*) as total_student from student1 group by class;
+-------+---------------+
| class | total_student |
+-------+---------------+
|    10 |             5 |
|     9 |             5 |
+-------+---------------+



Find the highest marks scored in Math.

mysql> select max(marks) as math_highest_marks from student1 where subject="math";

+--------------------+
|              92.00 |
