BANK ACCOUNT AND CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

A bank wants to maintain customer and account information in MySQL. You have been asked to design a bank_account table that strictly enforces the bank's data rules using NOT NULL, UNIQUE and CHECK constraints.

CREATE THE BANK_ACCOUNT TABLE

Create a table named bank_account with the following columns:

account_id
INT
Account ID, Primary Key, Auto Increment

customer_name
VARCHAR(50)
Mandatory

email
VARCHAR(100)
Mandatory and must be unique

phone
VARCHAR(15)
Mandatory and must be unique

account_type
VARCHAR(20)
Mandatory

age
INT
Mandatory, customer must be between 18 and 70

balance
DECIMAL(12,2)
Mandatory, balance cannot be negative

monthly_income
DECIMAL(12,2)
Mandatory, monthly income must be greater than 10000

experience
INT
Mandatory, experience must be between 0 and 40 years

branch_city
VARCHAR(30)
Mandatory

status
VARCHAR(20)
Mandatory, only Active, Inactive or Frozen

interest_rate
DECIMAL(5,2)
Mandatory, cannot be negative

opening_year
INT
Mandatory

APPLY THE CONSTRAINTS

NOT NULL CONSTRAINTS

The following columns cannot contain NULL:

customer_name
email
phone
account_type
age
balance
monthly_income
experience
branch_city
status
interest_rate
opening_year

UNIQUE CONSTRAINTS

The following values must be unique across customers:

email
phone

Two customers cannot have the same email or phone number.

CHECK CONSTRAINTS ON INDIVIDUAL COLUMNS

Age must be between 18 and 70.
Balance must be greater than or equal to 0.
Monthly income must be greater than 10000.
Experience must be between 0 and 40 years.
Interest rate must be greater than or equal to 0.
Status can only be Active, Inactive or Frozen.
Opening year must be between 2015 and 2026.
COMBINATION CHECK CONSTRAINTS

Rule 1: Account Type and Minimum Balance

The bank allows the following minimum balances:

Savings → minimum balance 1000

Current → minimum balance 5000

Premium → minimum balance 25000

Examples:

Savings + 2000 is valid.

Savings + 500 is invalid.

Current + 7000 is valid.

Current + 3000 is invalid.

Premium + 30000 is valid.

Premium + 15000 is invalid.

Implement this using a CHECK constraint involving account_type and balance.

Rule 2: Age and Account Type

Customers below 21 years can only have a Savings account.

Examples:

Age 19 and Savings is valid.

Age 19 and Current is invalid.

Age 19 and Premium is invalid.

Age 30 and Current is valid.

Age 30 and Premium is valid.

Implement this using a CHECK constraint involving age and account_type.

Rule 3: Monthly Income and Account Type

Customers must have an appropriate monthly income:

Savings → income must be at least 15000.

Current → income must be at least 30000.

Premium → income must be at least 75000.

Examples:

Savings + 20000 is valid.

Savings + 12000 is invalid.

Current + 40000 is valid.

Current + 20000 is invalid.

Premium + 90000 is valid.

Premium + 50000 is invalid.

Implement this using a CHECK constraint involving account_type and monthly_income.

Rule 4: Experience and Age

A customer cannot have more experience than realistically possible based on their age.

Experience must be less than or equal to age minus 18.

Examples:

Age 25 and Experience 7 is valid.

Age 25 and Experience 10 is invalid.

Age 40 and Experience 20 is valid.

Age 40 and Experience 25 is invalid.

Implement this using a CHECK constraint involving age and experience.

Rule 5: Status and Balance

Business rules:

Active accounts must have balance greater than 0.

Inactive accounts must have balance equal to 0.

Frozen accounts can have a balance, but it must be at least 1000.

Examples:

Active + 5000 is valid.

Active + 0 is invalid.

Inactive + 0 is valid.

Inactive + 5000 is invalid.

Frozen + 3000 is valid.

Frozen + 500 is invalid.

Implement this using a CHECK constraint involving status and balance.

Rule 6: Opening Year and Experience

Assume the current year is 2026.

Customer experience cannot be greater than the number of years since the account was opened.

Therefore:

experience must be less than or equal to 2026 - opening_year.

Examples:

Opening Year 2020 and Experience 6 is valid.

Opening Year 2020 and Experience 10 is invalid.

Opening Year 2024 and Experience 2 is valid.

Opening Year 2024 and Experience 5 is invalid.

INSERT VALID RECORDS

Insert at least 10 valid customers satisfying all constraints simultaneously.

Your records should cover:

Different age groups

Different experience levels

Different income ranges

Different balance amounts

All three statuses

Different interest rates

Different opening years

Make sure email and phone numbers are unique.
